<p>One app for your whole self-hosted download stack: Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, Bazarr, qBittorrent, autobrr and Cleanuparr.</p>
<ul>
<li>Radarr movies and Sonarr series with search, monitoring, renaming and bulk edits</li>
<li>Calendar of upcoming movies and episodes</li>
<li>Activity: queues with manual import, qBittorrent torrents and history</li>
<li>Add torrents from magnet links and .torrent files</li>
<li>Bazarr subtitles in any language for every movie and episode</li>
<li>Prowlarr, autobrr and Cleanuparr tools, and System health with disk space warnings</li>
<li>Notifications through ntfy, with the app's own background checks as a fallback</li>
<li>Upcoming and Downloads widgets, and a tile for qBittorrent's alternative speed limits</li>
<li>Several servers of each type, imported by paste or QR code</li>
<li>Self-signed certificates trusted by fingerprint, encrypted API keys</li>
</ul>
<p>Unofficial app, not affiliated with any of these projects.</p>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
